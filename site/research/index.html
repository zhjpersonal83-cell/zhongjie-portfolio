Questions and interests

The questions I want to investigate.

01

Reliable visual segmentation under environmental and sensor shift

When should a visual segmentation prediction be accepted or flagged as unreliable under changing conditions?

A proposed doctoral direction: investigate whether confidence calibration, feature novelty and perturbation sensitivity help identify unreliable segmentation predictions when data conditions change. These are future research questions, not completed experiments.

02

Medical image analysis

When do better image-processing outputs lead to more reliable measurements and useful predictions?

Cell segmentation, quantitative morphology, evaluation groups, domain differences and the relationship between upstream processing and downstream utility.

03

Emotion, context & multimodal evidence

How should a model use context and communicate uncertainty when observable signals do not uniquely determine emotion?

Building from facial-expression work towards questions about dialogue, contextual information, explanations and carefully bounded interpretation.

04

Reliable AI systems

What evidence is needed before a model result can support a decision or a software workflow?

Baseline comparisons, reproducible evaluation, failure analysis, transparent uncertainty and practical interfaces for inspecting results.

05

Computational modelling

How can mechanistic assumptions and measured data be connected without confusing model fit with explanation?

An additional area of interest informed by coursework and literature reading: individual-based models, sensitivity, uncertainty and multiscale reasoning.
