MSc dissertation · Oral histology

Cell segmentation & downstream evidence

Comparing four segmentation pipelines to understand how cell measurements and prediction results change with the method used.

← All projects

Dissertation title: Automated Extraction of Quantitative Data from Histology Images

The question

How much do downstream quantitative measurements and predictions depend on the cell-segmentation pipeline? The dissertation studied oral H&E histology, separating segmentation quality, morphology measurements and predictive utility.

What I did

Compared QuPath, Cellpose-DINO, Cyto R-CNN and CSGO pipelines, examined their quantitative outputs, and assessed downstream models with attention to grouping and the independence of evaluation data.

What the result supports

The dissertation did not establish a clear gain from fusion over the image-only approach. This makes the distinction between a better segmentation output, more useful measurements and better predictions central to the interpretation.

Research practice

The work emphasised comparable pipelines, traceable processing, grouped evaluation and explicit limitations. The portfolio presents a professional summary of that work; the dissertation itself and research data are not distributed here.
