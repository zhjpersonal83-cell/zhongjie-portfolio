Personal software project · When to trust AI and when to ask a person

Marginloom - AI reliability workbench

An interactive workspace for understanding how much to trust AI predictions. Explore mistakes, compare when human review helps, record review notes and export the evidence behind a decision.

← All projectsLaunch application ↗View source on GitHub

The question

When is an AI prediction reliable enough to use, and when should a person check it? Marginloom helps you inspect mistakes and see the evidence behind that choice.

What you can do

Choose an included example or import prediction results. Explore where the model is confident or often wrong, compare different rules for human review, record notes and export a report. A separate illustrative viewer shows how image-analysis results can be inspected.

Keep decisions understandable

Separate workspaces keep evaluations and review notes organised. Saved settings and reports help you revisit which choices were made and why.

Evidence and scope

The public project includes example models, evaluation material and documented checks for calculations, separate workspaces, review notes and exports. Included examples and the image viewer use synthetic data.

Status

Outputs & learning

  • A practical way to explore AI mistakes and decide when human review is helpful.
  • Review notes, saved decision settings and exportable evidence reports.
  • Public source and documented examples for revisiting the work.

Scope & limitations

  • A personal prototype developed with AI assistance, not evidence of a commercial business or customer deployment.
  • Demonstration datasets are generated or synthetic; no clinical validation or external production benchmark is claimed.
  • Historical checks do not certify every later change; browser file-saving and production readiness retain documented limitations.

Evidence basis

Public project source, example models and documented checks support the evaluation, human-review and reporting workflow.
