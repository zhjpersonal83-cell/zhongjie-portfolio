Home/Privacy

Privacy

A small, transparent public website.

What this site stores, what its links do and which materials are made public.

Local preferences

The theme preference is stored in your browser when storage is available. Course and project filters can appear in the current page URL so a view can be shared. This build contains no analytics scripts, advertising trackers, account system or contact-form backend.

Email and external links

Email links open your chosen email application. GitHub, LinkedIn and reference links open external sites with their own policies. No contact form submits a message through this website.

Public materials

The professional profile, project summaries, portrait, public CV and original BSc certificate are included. Detailed transcripts, account information, private repositories and raw research data are not part of the public build.

Hosting

The site is static. A hosting provider or server administrator may keep ordinary request logs; the contents and retention of those logs depend on the eventual server configuration.

Attribution

University, dataset, framework and software names identify the subjects discussed and do not imply endorsement or affiliation. Project status and limitations appear on each case study. Rights in third-party material remain with their owners.
