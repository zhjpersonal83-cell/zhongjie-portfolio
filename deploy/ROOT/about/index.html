About

A profile built around the work.

I work across computer vision, machine learning evaluation and software development. My projects connect model experiments with usable systems, with particular attention to what the evidence supports and where it stops.

I earned a BSc in Artificial Intelligence and Computer Science at the University of Sheffield in July 2025. I then studied its MSc Artificial Intelligence programme in 2025–2026. My MSc dissertation has been submitted; final results and the degree award remain pending as of 16 October 2026.

My undergraduate project combined a facial-expression classifier with a Java/Tomcat web application. My MSc dissertation examined how different cell-segmentation pipelines affect quantitative measurements and downstream prediction in oral histology.

Alongside academic work, I develop software prototypes and maintain this bilingual portfolio. I am exploring doctoral study and suitable research or engineering opportunities, bringing together implementation, careful evaluation and clear technical communication.
